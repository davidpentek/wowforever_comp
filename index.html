Buffs and tools by level

Road to 60
From level 10 together: zones, quests, and every dungeon once. Dungeons take five, so one of you sits out or swaps in each run.

How it scores: each damage spec gets points from its MythicSim tier (a single-target boss sim), group buffs add points per teammate they help, and every pair of players loses points for sharing armor type, stat priority and weapon types. The numbers are a planning index, not simulated DPS. Race and class combinations and racials are from the WoW Forever beta (September 2026); buffs and armor rules follow Classic and may shift before launch. Icons are Blizzard game art. Crafts, spell levels, zones and quests follow Classic, with WoW Forever's announced changes and nine new dungeons marked; expect some to move during beta.
